Indigenous Futures Initiative

Student-led education, STEM, and community projects

About us

Building opportunities through education and technology

Indigenous Futures Initiative is a student-led organization based in the Bay Area that works with Native communities to create projects based on community needs. We support communities through tutoring, STEM, engineering, technology, and other educational opportunities.

What we offer

Tutoring & Academic Support

  • Math, science, and writing support
  • Homework help
  • Individual and group tutoring

STEM & Engineering

  • Coding and computer science
  • Engineering activities
  • Technology projects

Test Preparation

  • SAT preparation
  • ACT preparation
  • AP exam support

Legacy Projects

  • Oral histories
  • Legacy books
  • Community storytelling

Interested in working with us?

We work directly with Native communities and organizations to build projects around community needs.
